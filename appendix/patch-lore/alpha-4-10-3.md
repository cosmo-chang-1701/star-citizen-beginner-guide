## 尼克斯星系剜度 (Vanduul) 攻擊持續升級

<!-- **IS THE ONGOING TERRAFORMATION OF NYX I TO BLAME FOR THE INCREASE IN VANDUUL CLAN ACTIVITY?** -->

> **尼克斯 I (Nyx I) 正在進行的地球化改造是否該為剜度氏族活動的增加負責？**

<!-- While at first attributed to recent Naval actions along the front lines, the continued increase of Vanduul attacks across the Nyx system have led some experts to theorize that the ongoing terraformation of Nyx I could be the cause. Numerous corporations have begun to set up operations to take advantage of the planet's untold amounts of previously inaccessible resources that wait beneath the surface. Those with knowledge of the Vanduul's previous incursions into Human system have noticed that their attacks often are drawn to areas of high resource extraction. -->

雖然起初將其歸咎於近期前線的海軍行動，但遍布整個尼克斯星系的剜度 (Vanduul) 攻擊持續增加，已使一些專家推測正在進行的尼克斯 I (Nyx I) 地球化改造可能是罪魁禍首。眾多企業已開始設立營運據點，以利用地表之下等待開採、以往難以取得的龐大資源。那些了解剜度先前入侵人類星系歷史的人士注意到，他們的攻擊往往會被高資源開採區域所吸引。

然而不可否認的是，非法組織「碎刃幫」(Shattered Blade) 持續挑釁這支外星勢力，因此有興趣協助持續對抗這場危機的人員，應聯繫正積極招募的互安防衛 (InterSec Defense Solutions)。

## 薊花·珀金斯 (Thistle Perkins) 獲撤銷指控

<!-- **THE GUILD MEMBER, ACCUSED OF WILLFUL DESTRUCTION OF PROPERTY, HAS BEEN CLEARED OF ALL WRONGDOING.** -->

> **該名遭指控故意破壞財產的公會成員，已獲洗清所有罪嫌。**

<!-- When hired to cripple the operational effectiveness of an outlaw group supposedly targeting her client, Thistle Perkins found herself arrested after destroying several servers that turned out to be owned by the Teardown Tech Services Group. With huge ramifications for how much operational responsibility mercenaries have when contracted for a job, guild members everywhere were closely watching this case. Claiming that the responsibility lay with her employer for providing falsified records, the High Advocate agreed and have thankfully dismissed all charges. -->

在受雇削弱一個據稱以其客戶為目標的非法組織之運作效能時，薊花·珀金斯 (Thistle Perkins) 摧毀了數台伺服器，結果卻發現這些伺服器屬於拆解科技服務集團 (Teardown Tech Services Group)，隨後遭到逮捕。鑑於傭兵在簽署工作合約時應承擔多少作業責任具有重大深遠影響，各地的公會成員都高度關注此案。珀金斯主張其雇主提供造假紀錄應承擔全責，倡導局高等法官 (The High Advocate) 對此表示認同，並慶幸地撤銷了所有指控。

## 中毒疫情源頭追溯至受污染酸黃瓜

<!-- **A FAVORITE SNACK OF MANY MERCENARIES HAS BEEN IDENTIFIED AS THE SOURCE OF RECENT FOOD POISONING SURGE.** -->

> **許多傭兵喜愛的點心被確認為近期食物中毒激增的源頭。**

<!-- Lying in the hospital having barely survived their ordeal, Thomas Matthewson of New Austin, a guild member and long-time self-proclaimed picklephile, has been bed-ridden for over two weeks following a bout of nearly fatal food poisoning. Matthewson, like over a dozen others, had unknowingly eaten a pickle tainted with trace levels of an industrial coolant, Bitulanye-LT-40. Known to cause disruptions to the nervous system, UEE Health Service investigators are still trying to piece together how the pickles were exposed but have issued a full product recall. -->

來自新奧斯汀 (New Austin) 的公會成員、長年自詡為酸黃瓜狂熱者的托馬斯·馬修森 (Thomas Matthewson)，在一場險些致命的食物中毒後已臥床超過兩週，在醫院裡歷經磨難才勉強存活。馬修森與十多名其他人一樣，在不知情的情況下食用了受微量工業冷卻劑 Bitulanye-LT-40 污染的酸黃瓜。已知該冷卻劑會對神經系統造成損害，聯合地球帝國衛生署 (UEE Health Service) 調查人員仍在試圖拼湊酸黃瓜是如何遭受污染的經過，但已發布了全面的產品召回令。
